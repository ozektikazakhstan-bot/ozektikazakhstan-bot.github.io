Every day we publish five news updates at 08:00, 11:00, 14:00, 18:00 and 21:00 (Astana time): morning news, economy, society, the main story of the day and the evening digest.

How we work

We select important stories from public Kazakhstan news sources, summarise them in our own words, always credit the original source, and an editor reviews posts before publication.
